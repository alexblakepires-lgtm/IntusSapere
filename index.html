intussapere

Latin. To know from within. The phrase sits beside Hahnemann's aude sapere, dare to know, which opens the Organon.

We build careful tools for homeopathic practice, made with practitioners rather than at them.

What we're making

A conversational guide to homeopathy. It listens, recognizes patterns, and helps organize what is spoken, pointing inquiry toward the remedy that fits.

It teaches rather than prescribes. One version is being built for practitioners and their students, another for anyone curious enough to ask a first question. Both are in development now.

Trying it early

A private beta is running with a small group of practitioners. We are keeping it small on purpose, because the clinical standard matters more to us than the headcount.

If you teach, practice, or study homeopathy and want to see it, write to us and we will tell you where it stands.
